Stephanie Maaz

Algorithms for changing systems.

I am Stephanie Maaz, a researcher in theoretical computer science / mathematics. I work on algorithms, with a current focus on dynamic and sequential settings where solutions have to adapt rather than simply exist.

In 2025, I completed my PhD at the University of Waterloo in the Algorithms and Complexity Group, advised by Naomi Nishimura and Amer E. Mouawad.

Email Google Scholar DBLP GitHub ORCID
Computing a solution is only half the story. In many settings, whether relatively static or highly dynamic, and whether geometric, graph-based, or otherwise, the solution must also be repaired or reconfigured cost-effectively.
