CV Thesis

Salut , I recently completed (2021-25) my PhD at the University of Waterloo in the Algorithms and Complexity Group, where I worked on reconfiguration (transforming one solution into another via steps while maintaining feasibility) and solution discovery (repairing an infeasible configuration into a valid solution with minimal change), with a focus on parameterized complexity, structural graph parameters, kernelization, and logic-based meta-theorems. My supervisors were Naomi Nishimura and Amer E. Mouawad. I am currently on the job market.
